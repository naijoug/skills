# Validate the offer

Express the hypothesis as: for this audience, this asset/service produces this
outcome, and this observable action would support demand.

Assess pain, buyer clarity, reachable audience, proof, delivery capacity and trust
using concrete evidence. Scores may help compare ideas, but no numeric threshold
establishes demand. Prefer a manual or thin proof vehicle when it can test the
outcome before a product investment.

A flexible short sprint covers audience/problem evidence, one proof asset,
authorized distribution, objections, a narrowly scoped offer and a decision.
Include price/refund terms only within the requested planning or selling scope.

| Signal | What it supports |
| --- | --- |
| Reads, likes, saves | Attention; not purchase intent |
| Concrete pain story or existing workaround | Problem recognition |
| Qualified inquiry, sample request, booked discovery call | Intent; not payment |
| Deposit, pre-order or paid pilot | Payment evidence; delivery quality remains to be proven |

Log source, observed behavior, interpretation and next check. Use the user's
measurement window; if unspecified, propose a reasonable window without treating
it as a missing authorization. Do not invent conversion rates or treat silence
from a tiny audience as a definitive market verdict.

For written assets, prepare only the hook, summary, one CTA and measurement fields
needed for the chosen channel. Stop adding variants once the experiment is ready
to collect the next evidence.
