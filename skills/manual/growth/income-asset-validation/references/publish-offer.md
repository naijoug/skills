# Single-channel publication

A launch-preparation request authorizes drafts and checks. A publish/send request
must identify, or unambiguously inherit, the channel, account, content/offer and
intended audience. Reuse existing authority; availability of a tool is not consent.

Prepare the final draft, delivery boundary, price if applicable, one CTA and a
working contact path. Resolve material missing content or account decisions before
publishing; continue independent local preparation while waiting. A missing
observation-window preference alone is not a publication blocker.

Select one channel for the experiment unless the user explicitly chooses a wider
scope. Before the external action, check the final promise against what can be
delivered and the action against the authorized account/destination. Follow the
host's permission rules. Do not invent contacts, testimonials or demand signals.

After an authorized publication, verify and record its URL/receipt, time, account,
actual content and next observation window. If the outcome is unknown, inspect for
an existing post before retrying so the experiment does not create duplicates.

Record replies, inquiries and payment signals distinctly. Do not promise ongoing
monitoring or schedule follow-ups unless requested and actually configured. A user
correction or broken contact path can justify an earlier revision; the observation
window is not a rule forbidding necessary fixes.
