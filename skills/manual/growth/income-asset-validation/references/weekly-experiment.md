# Choose one weekly experiment

Use the available time, reachable audience, delivery capacity and existing inputs
to choose one experiment. Compare a few viable candidates only when selection is
unresolved; do not fill a standard candidate board for a clear request.

Prefer a small artifact that answers a real question: a reproducible tutorial,
a narrowly scoped contribution, a workflow tool, or a service sample. State:

- Audience, pain and falsifiable hypothesis.
- Smallest deliverable and non-goals.
- Verification method and the stronger demand signal still needed.
- Distribution channel, if available and authorized.
- Continue/narrow/stop conditions and today's next action.

When execution is requested, build and verify the slice, record actual results and
leave it uncommitted unless a commit is authorized. With no external channel,
label local work as preparation; repeated self-use may support personal utility
but does not prove a paying market. Shrink oversized experiments instead of
building infrastructure before evidence justifies it.
